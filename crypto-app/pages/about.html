About & Help

Learn what FrostCoin does and how to use each feature.

What is FrostCoin?

FrostCoin is a cryptocurrency price monitoring system that helps you track live prices, analyze market trends with technical indicators, forecast future movements, and manage your portfolio — all in one place. Built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, it runs entirely in your browser with no signup required.

What you can do

Live prices

Track real-time prices, 24-hour changes, and market data for the top 50 cryptocurrencies. Auto-refreshes every minute.

Interactive charts

Line and candlestick views with zoom, pan, drawing tools, and annotations. Choose from 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, or 1-year ranges.

Technical indicators

Built-in moving averages (MA20, MA50), RSI, MACD, and volume bars to help identify market momentum and trends.

Price prediction

Two statistical forecasting models — linear regression and moving average — with side-by-side accuracy comparison.

Portfolio manager

Track your holdings, calculate live profit and loss, and visualize asset allocation with an interactive donut chart.

Light & dark mode

Switch between light and dark themes anytime. Your preference is remembered for next time you visit.

How to use

Tracking a cryptocurrency

  1. Open the Dashboard The main page shows the top 50 cryptocurrencies sorted by market cap.
  2. Use the search bar Type a coin name (e.g. “Bitcoin”) or symbol (e.g. “BTC”) to filter the list.
  3. Click on a coin Click any row to open the detailed view with charts, indicators, and market data.

Reading the chart

  1. Choose a time range Use the 24H / 7D / 30D / 90D / 1Y buttons in the chart toolbar.
  2. Switch chart type Click “Line” for a simple price line, or “Candles” to see open/high/low/close candlesticks.
  3. Zoom and pan Scroll on the chart to zoom in and out. Click and drag to pan across time.
  4. Add moving averages Click MA 20 or MA 50 in the toolbar to overlay moving average lines.
  5. Use drawing tools The drawing buttons let you mark trend lines, horizontal support / resistance levels, and annotations directly on the chart. Click the × button to clear all drawings.

Managing your portfolio

  1. Open the Portfolio page Click “Portfolio” in the navigation menu.
  2. Add a holding Click “+ Add holding”. Search for a coin, enter the amount you own and the price you paid per coin.
  3. View profit & loss The summary cards show total value, total cost, and your live P/L. The donut chart shows how your portfolio is allocated.
  4. Edit or delete a holding Each row has pencil and trash icons to update or remove it.
  5. Reset everything The “Reset” button at the top wipes all holdings (this cannot be undone).

Running price predictions

  1. Open the Prediction page Click “Prediction” in the navigation menu.
  2. Pick a cryptocurrency Choose any coin from the dropdown.
  3. Configure the forecast Choose how much history to use, how far ahead to forecast, and the moving average window size.
  4. Click “Run prediction” Both models run together. The chart shows historical data plus both forecast lines.
  5. Compare the models The accuracy cards (MAE, RMSE, MAPE) show which model fit the past data best. Lower numbers mean smaller errors.

Frequently asked questions

  • RSI (Relative Strength Index) is a momentum indicator that ranges from 0 to 100. Traditionally, an RSI above 70 suggests the asset may be overbought (price has risen too fast and could reverse), while an RSI below 30 suggests it may be oversold (price has fallen too fast and could bounce). Values between 30 and 70 indicate neutral momentum.
  • MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) compares a fast exponential moving average (12 periods) with a slow one (26 periods). When MACD is above its signal line (9-period EMA of MACD), momentum is generally bullish. When MACD is below the signal line, momentum is generally bearish.
  • The two models in FrostCoin (linear regression and moving average) are simple statistical forecasts based on historical price data only. They cannot account for news, regulation, sentiment, or other real-world events that drive cryptocurrency prices. The accuracy metrics (MAE, RMSE, MAPE) shown on the prediction page measure how well each model fits past data — not how well it will predict the future. These predictions are not financial advice.
  • All portfolio data is stored locally in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser will remove your portfolio. There is no signup, no account, and no cloud sync.
  • All cryptocurrency data comes from the CoinGecko public API. CoinGecko aggregates data from hundreds of exchanges and updates roughly every 1–2 minutes. FrostCoin caches API responses for 60 seconds to stay within free-tier rate limits.
  • A few common causes: (1) you’re offline — FrostCoin needs internet to fetch live prices; (2) the CoinGecko free API is temporarily rate-limited — wait a minute and refresh; (3) your browser is blocking the request — try a different browser or check the developer console for errors.
  • These are accuracy metrics that measure how close a model’s predictions were to the actual prices. MAE (Mean Absolute Error) is the average absolute difference in dollars. RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) is similar but penalizes large errors more heavily. MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) expresses the error as a percentage of the actual price, which is useful when comparing different coins. Lower numbers indicate better fit.
  • No. FrostCoin is a monitoring and analysis tool only. You cannot buy, sell, or transfer cryptocurrency through this site. The portfolio feature is for tracking purposes — you record what you already own elsewhere.
