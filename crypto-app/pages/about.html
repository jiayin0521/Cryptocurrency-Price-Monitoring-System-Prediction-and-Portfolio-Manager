What is CrypCoin?

A browser-based cryptocurrency monitoring system — track live prices, analyse charts with technical indicators, forecast trends, and manage your portfolio. No signup, no backend, runs entirely in your browser.

What you can do

Live prices

Track real-time prices, 24-hour changes, and market data for the top 50 cryptocurrencies. Auto-refreshes every minute.

Interactive charts

Line and candlestick views with zoom, pan, drawing tools, and annotations. Choose from 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, or 1-year ranges.

Technical indicators

Built-in moving averages (MA20, MA50), RSI, MACD, and volume bars to help identify market momentum and trends.

Price prediction

Two statistical forecasting models — linear regression and moving average — with side-by-side accuracy comparison.

Portfolio manager

Track your holdings, calculate live profit and loss, and visualize asset allocation with an interactive donut chart.

Light & dark mode

Switch between light and dark themes anytime. Your preference is remembered for next time you visit.

Frequently asked questions

  • All data comes from the CoinGecko public API, which aggregates prices from hundreds of exchanges and updates every 1–2 minutes.
  • Linear regression and moving average are simple statistical models based on historical data only. They cannot account for news or market sentiment. Not financial advice.
  • All portfolio data is stored locally in your browser via localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. No signup, no account, no cloud sync.

Built with

CrypCoin is built using only client-side web technologies. No server, no database, no backend.
